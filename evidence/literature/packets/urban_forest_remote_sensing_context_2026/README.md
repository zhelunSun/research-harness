## Non-goals

This packet does not establish a universal urban-forest workflow, an Agent method, or an effect of knowledge representation or automated planning. It does not replace the opening report's `[REF-MISSING]` markers or change the Chapter 1--3 method definitions. Its narrower role is to make the domain-dependency argument auditable: observed remote-sensing products depend on data sources, scale, processing and validation, while ecological or planning conclusions add use-, place-, and discipline-specific interpretation.
