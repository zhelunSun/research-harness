## Non-goals

This packet does not show that one-shot planning, implicit state, or outcome self-evaluation is inadequate in every scientific-agent system. It also does not establish that explicit workflow state, feedback loops, or multi-agent roles improve an urban-forest remote-sensing Agent. Its narrower role is to identify four source-specific mechanisms and their reported capability boundaries so that the opening report cannot turn frontier demonstrations into a general field claim.
